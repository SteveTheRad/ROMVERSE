# ROMVERSE design vision and polish goal

## Goal

Transform ROMVERSE Desktop and Companion into a coherent, premium Android engineering universe
that feels unmistakably ROMVERSE at first glance and remains effortless to understand under real
pressure. Every page should reveal the current truth, the best next action, its consequence, and
the evidence that will prove the outcome. The experience should delight beginners without hiding
power from experts, use purposeful cinematic motion without compromising accessibility, and make
the creator, RadForge Labs, official destinations, supporters, and product philosophy feel like one
authentic story.

This visual work must never weaken technical truth. Animation cannot imply progress that was not
measured, colour cannot convert unknown evidence into success, and a polished control cannot grant
an operation that its owning subsystem does not support.

## Product design direction

- **Living Universe:** deep-space surfaces, cyan evidence signals, violet creative energy, green
  verified outcomes, amber human attention, and red only for real danger or a stopped boundary.
- **One mission at a time:** one visually dominant next action per page. Secondary tools sit behind
  clear disclosure, never in a wall of equal buttons.
- **Guided and Architect:** Guided Mode presents the shortest safe route and folds supporting
  evidence. Architect Mode reveals complete technical evidence and specialist tools while retaining
  exact-target, artifact-integrity, and command-validity checks.
- **Responsive by construction:** every primary workflow must remain readable without horizontal
  scrolling at the supported minimum desktop size. Dense cards reflow, controls stack, text wraps,
  and dialogs open at a stable readable size.
- **Evidence-aware colour:** state colour always accompanies plain-language text and never stands
  alone as the only status indicator.

## Layout system

1. A restrained global shell owns navigation, workspace context, connection state, and mode.
2. Every product page begins with a short identity header and one sentence explaining its purpose.
3. A mission strip or compact state summary shows where the user is in any multi-step journey.
4. A primary cockpit answers four questions in order: What is connected? What did ROMVERSE find?
   What should I do next? How will ROMVERSE verify the result?
5. Supporting evidence, history, raw details, export, and specialist utilities follow the primary
   decision and are collapsible in Guided Mode.
6. Empty, error, blocked, and disconnected states must retain the same layout so controls do not
   jump or disappear unpredictably.

## Theme and visual quality

- Consolidate spacing, type scale, radii, border hierarchy, status treatments, icon sizes, and
  button roles into shared design tokens instead of page-specific approximations.
- Use dimensional gradients and glow only to reinforce hierarchy. Keep reading surfaces calm and
  high contrast.
- Preserve the byte-exact approved ROMVERSE and RadForge Labs masters. Place them inside composed
  identity stages so neither logo looks pasted onto the interface.
- Give every theme a complete semantic palette rather than changing only accent borders.
- Verify light-on-dark contrast, focus rings, keyboard order, text scaling, and high-DPI rendering.

## Motion language

- Use 160–220 ms fades or spatial handoffs for state changes and 320–520 ms transitions only when
  they explain page or workflow continuity.
- Animate measured progress from real producer events only. Indeterminate work stays indeterminate.
- Pause decorative effects when a page is hidden and honour Desktop Reduced Motion, Companion
  Reduced Effects, and the operating system animator setting.
- Never use looping motion beside warnings, consent text, terminal output, or destructive choices.

## About the creator and official links

The About experience should act as a polished founder and trust hub:

- approved ROMVERSE and RadForge Labs identities presented as one connected brand story;
- a dedicated SteveTheRad founder spotlight with the reason ROMVERSE exists;
- current workspace accomplishments derived from real local evidence, never marketing counters;
- verified links for `romverse.app`, `radforgelabs.com`, Facebook `ROMVERSEOfficial`, Reddit
  `u/ROMVERSE`, and the `SteveTheRad/ROMVERSE` GitHub repository;
- future social destinations added only after their exact official URLs are supplied and verified;
- clear Terms, Privacy, licences, release provenance, and honest capability boundaries.

## Easter egg: ROMVERSE — The Origin

Double-clicking the approved ROMVERSE logo opens an optional comic-universe cinematic. A portal
pulls in a starfield, halftone panels and inked frames introduce each chapter, energy-burst captions
punctuate the story, and the approved logo emerges from the event horizon. The finale delivers a
heartfelt message to supporters, testers, builders, curious newcomers, and everyone who shared an
idea. The sequence includes pause, previous, next, skip, replay, close, optional sound, and reduced-
motion controls. Reduced Motion presents the same complete story as a stable illustrated reveal.

## Delivery waves

1. Establish the system in the Recovery cockpit, project-creation dialog, About founder hub, and
   Origin comic sequence.
2. Apply the hierarchy and responsive rules to ROM Studio and Genesis so build work cannot loop or
   end in a dead page.
3. Unify Recovery Bay, Guardian, Device Atlas, Archives, Live Console, and Time Machine around the
   same mission and evidence patterns.
4. Bring Companion navigation, pairing, Vitalis, Lifeline, About, and device status to visual and
   functional parity with Desktop.
5. Complete cross-platform visual regression, keyboard, high-DPI, minimum-size, reduced-motion,
   performance, and representative real-device acceptance testing.

## Definition of done

- No clipped titles, overlapping controls, unreadable states, dead-end buttons, false green states,
  duplicate primary actions, or unexpected horizontal scroll in supported layouts.
- Every action either works, explains exactly why it is unavailable, or routes to the evidence and
  next resolution that can make it available.
- Guided and Architect modes are visually distinct, predictable, and fully tested.
- The same device/build/finding identity survives navigation, repair handoff, and fresh outcome
  verification; a different phone can never satisfy the comparison.
- Every animation has a purpose, stops when hidden, and has a complete reduced-motion equivalent.
- Desktop and Companion packages reproduce from source, pass their full automated suites, receive
  visual inspection, and retain honest release boundaries before public signing or publication.
