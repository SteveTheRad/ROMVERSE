# ROMVERSE Product Vision

## Purpose

ROMVERSE is intended to make Android ROM building, diagnosis, customization, recovery preparation, and flashing understandable for beginners while remaining useful to experienced users.

The product is designed as a first-class PC application paired with an Android Companion. ROMVERSE should present real evidence, real progress, clear warnings, and recoverable workflows rather than hiding complex operations behind unexplained automation.

## Product identity

**ROMVERSE — Build. Optimize. Perfect.**  
Created by SteveTheRad — A RadForge Labs Project.

## Product principles

- Beginner-friendly without becoming technically shallow.
- Evidence-backed decisions rather than unsupported certainty.
- Guardian-enforced safety for meaningful or destructive operations.
- Preserve projects, firmware, backups, configuration, and user data by default.
- Prefer genuinely safe automatic fixes; require user authority for elevated privilege, destructive actions, rooting, unlocking, flashing, or other material choices.
- Never relock a bootloader.
- Never upload raw personal data; sanitize unnecessary identifiers and logs.
- Show real progress for long-running operations.
- Keep startup responsive; do not delay normal application launch merely to show branding.
- Maintain product documentation with the source so development is portable across machines.

## Online direction

ROMVERSE is online-first/online-required. Online intelligence may research current device and firmware information, obtain required ROM Studio tooling, diagnose problems, plan fixes, and verify outcomes. Sources, confidence, limitations, and progress must remain visible.

## Membership direction

Current commercial direction:

- A$14.99 AUD per month.
- Seven-day full-access trial.
- Month-to-month; cancel anytime.
- One membership includes the PC app, Companion, Nexus/online intelligence, Guardian, community/forums, updates, and normal current/future features.
- No annual plan at launch.
- No tiers.
- No paid add-ons.
- No ads or sponsored placements.

## Repository continuity requirement

This public repository is the authoritative technical source of truth for ROMVERSE. Documentation and source should eventually allow a developer to clone the project onto another correctly configured machine and continue development without depending on one particular PC or chat history.

Secrets, credentials, signing material, production keys, and other sensitive values must remain securely stored outside Git.
