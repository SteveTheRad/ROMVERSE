# ROMVERSE 20-Stage Master Goal

Complete ROMVERSE as a production-ready Android ROM development, flashing, diagnosis, repair, optimisation, knowledge, collaboration and companion platform. Implement the following agreed stages in order. Preserve ROMVERSE's evidence-first rule: never claim that a build, flash, repair, test or release succeeded without genuine evidence.

## 1. ROM Studio Guided Flow 2.0

Redesign the core ROM Studio journey as one clear workspace: firmware on the left, connected device on the right, and a central compatibility verdict explaining why the pairing is safe, risky or incompatible. Replace circular “provide evidence” navigation with one obvious next action and expandable in-place blockers with direct solutions. Automatically scan Device DNA and detect upgrade or downgrade status. Present the journey as Scan → Match → Build → Verify → Backup → Flash → Diagnose, with polished stage transitions, meaningful progress and animated verification. Guided Mode must provide warnings and recovery choices without unnecessary lockouts. Expert Mode must remove Guardian restrictions after an explicit risk-acceptance dialog. Add automatic post-flash diagnosis and recovery assistance when booting fails.

## 2. Device Intelligence Hub

When a phone connects, automatically create a polished live device profile with an animated Device DNA scan; exact model, codename, chipset, region, bootloader, slots, partitions, Android and firmware versions; upgrade and downgrade compatibility verdicts; known device-specific flashing and recovery knowledge; live battery, storage, health and connection state; automatically matched Knowledge Archive results; and a timeline of previous scans, flashes and repairs. Provide a clear **Start ROM Mission** action that sends verified information directly to ROM Studio. Present the phone as a premium holographic device passport with information unfolding around it.

## 3. Recovery Autopilot

When a flash fails or a device bootloops, automatically enter recovery mode. Detect whether the device is in ADB, Fastboot, recovery, disconnected or another measurable state. Compare before-and-after Device DNA and partition evidence, identify likely failures, search the offline Knowledge Archives for device-specific solutions, and rank recovery routes by confidence and risk. Execute safe repairs only after confirmation. Support appropriate slot switching, image restoration, cache or data guidance and verified stock recovery. Maintain an incident timeline of every action and result. Never report repaired until the device successfully boots and reconnects. Visually transition from an amber/red emergency command centre back to green/cyan as recovery checkpoints are genuinely verified.

## 4. ROMVERSE Synapse — Offline Knowledge Brain

Create one intelligent knowledge system supporting every feature. Store and search verified manufacturer documentation, trusted technical sources and useful community solutions. Match answers automatically using Device DNA, firmware, partition and error information. Include guides, known fixes, flashing procedures, compatibility reports and recovery paths for Android brands and devices. Label every record as official, verified, community-reported, experimental, disputed or outdated; retain its source, affected devices, success evidence and confidence. Learn only from confirmed ROMVERSE diagnoses and outcomes without collecting private user data. Detect conflicting advice, explain the safest answer, support signed knowledge updates and remain fully useful offline. Feed relevant answers into ROM Studio, Device Intelligence and Recovery Autopilot. Present Synapse as an animated cosmic neural archive of device constellations, knowledge galaxies and illuminated solution pathways.

## 7. ROM Simulation Lab — Device Digital Twin

Before touching hardware, rehearse an operation against a model derived from Device DNA. Detect missing images, mismatched codenames, downgrade risks and dangerous erase commands. Estimate storage, time and battery requirements. Preview what will be written, preserved or erased. Test rollback and recovery plans before enabling the real operation. Clearly state confidence and what cannot be proven without physical hardware. Visualise an exploded holographic phone with partitions lighting in sequence.
